FINANCIAL TRANSPARENCY

Understanding Your Coverage & Care

At D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC, we believe that quality behavioral healthcare should be accessible, transparent, and respectful.

Understanding health insurance coverage, benefits, and payment responsibilities is an essential part of establishing and continuing your care. Our team is dedicated to guiding you through insurance verification and answering billing questions promptly and clearly.

Professional billing administration and claims management for our organization are coordinated in partnership with I Work Efficient Billers to ensure accurate claim submission, diligent benefit verification, and responsive patient support.

COVERAGE OVERVIEW

Accepted Insurance & Coverage Verification

We work with a variety of insurance carriers and healthcare networks. Specific insurance carrier participation details and in-network agreements are currently being updated and verified with our billing partners.

Important Patient Notice:

Insurance participation and benefits must be verified before services are scheduled. Please contact our office for insurance verification and billing assistance.

Pre-Visit Verification

Before your initial evaluation, our team will review your health insurance details to confirm your active mental health and behavioral health benefits, telehealth coverage eligibility, and any applicable cost-sharing responsibilities.

Carrier Participation

Network participation and specific in-network health plans are verified individually. Please contact our administrative desk with your insurance details prior to booking to confirm real-time network status and benefit coverage.

I Work Efficient Billers

We collaborate with I Work Efficient Billers to manage billing inquiries, claims submission, and statement questions with professional precision and patient-focused care.

STEP-BY-STEP

How Insurance Verification Works

Follow these simple steps before your initial psychiatric evaluation to ensure smooth onboarding and clear billing expectations:

1

Provide Insurance Information

When requesting an appointment or completing your new patient intake form, provide clear front-and-back photos or copies of your insurance card and a valid photo ID.

2

Benefit & Telehealth Verification

In coordination with I Work Efficient Billers, our team verifies active eligibility, telehealth mental health benefits, and any applicable copay or deductible details.

3

Pre-Appointment Confirmation

Our administrative desk confirms your appointment time and reviews coverage information with you so there are no surprises on the day of your visit.

4

Ongoing Billing Support

For follow-up appointments, medication management visits, or billing questions, our team and billing partners remain available to assist you at every stage.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Billing Questions

Many insurance plans provide coverage for outpatient behavioral and mental health services delivered via secure telehealth. Coverage depends on your specific policy and state regulations. In coordination with I Work Efficient Billers, our team verifies your specific telehealth benefits prior to your appointment.

We recommend submitting your insurance information when scheduling your appointment or when completing your online patient intake form. Verifying your coverage in advance helps ensure seamless scheduling and avoids delays.

Please have a government-issued photo ID and your current insurance card (front and back). If the patient is a minor, the parent or legal guardian's ID and insurance card should be provided.

For any billing inquiries, claims questions, or statement clarifications, please contact our office desk at 240-917-6203 or email info@dahhealthsolutions.com. Billing support is coordinated in collaboration with I Work Efficient Billers.

Website Emergency & Crisis Disclaimer

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate medical or psychiatric emergency, is in immediate danger, or is at risk of harming themselves or another person, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

For mental health, suicide, or substance-use crisis support in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

* Do not use website contact forms, email, voicemail, or routine appointment requests for emergency situations.
